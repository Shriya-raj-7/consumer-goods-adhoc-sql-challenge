# Consumer Goods – Adhoc SQL Challenge

## Project Overview
This project is part of the **Codebasics Resume SQL Challenge**.  
The objective was to solve ad-hoc business requests using SQL and derive meaningful insights for decision-making.

The challenge is based on **AtliQ Hardwares**, an imaginary company that manufactures computer hardware and operates in multiple markets.

---

## Problem Statement
AtliQ Hardwares is one of the leading computer hardware producers in India and has expanded into other countries as well.

However, the management realized they were not getting enough insights to make quick and data-driven decisions.

To strengthen their analytics capability, the Data Analytics Director **Tony Sharma** decided to conduct a **SQL challenge** to evaluate candidates’ ability to solve business problems using data.

---

## Tools Used
- SQL
- MySQL Workbench
- Business Analysis

---

## Business Requests Solved

1. Identify the markets where **Atliq Exclusive** operates in the APAC region.
2. Calculate the **percentage increase in unique products from 2020 to 2021**.
3. Determine the **unique product count for each segment**.
4. Identify the **segment with the highest increase in products (2021 vs 2020)**.
5. Find the **products with the highest and lowest manufacturing costs**.
6. Identify the **top 5 customers receiving the highest average pre-invoice discount in India (FY 2021)**.
7. Generate a **monthly gross sales report for Atliq Exclusive**.
8. Identify the **quarter in 2020 with the highest total sold quantity**.
9. Determine **which sales channel contributed the highest gross sales in FY 2021**.
10. Identify the **top 3 products in each division based on sold quantity in FY 2021**.

---

## Key Insights

- Atliq Exclusive operates across **multiple APAC markets**.
- **Retail channel contributes the highest revenue (~73%)**.
- **Accessories segments outperform the PC division in sales**.
- **Q1 recorded the highest total sold quantity**.
- Strategic discounts are offered to maintain relationships with key customers.
